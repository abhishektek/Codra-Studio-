Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026 | Contact: abhishektekofficial@gmail.com

1. Information Handling

Codra Studio values your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

2. Locally Stored Project Files & AI Chats

Your source code, layouts, manifests, and project files remain stored locally on your device storage. We do not upload your private code or local AI conversation context to our servers.

3. Firebase Services

Codra Studio may use Google Firebase services solely for essential app configuration, crash analytics, and anonymous authentication where applicable.

4. Third-Party AI Services & API Keys

When you configure and use third-party AI providers, your queries are processed directly by that provider according to their own terms. All user-entered API keys remain stored locally on your device.

5. Security & User Rights

Reasonable measures are used within Android application sandboxes to protect data, though no digital storage can guarantee 100% security. You can clear or delete your local project data anytime by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
